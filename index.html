Talk to the AlpenBank voice assistant right here in your browser. No phone line, no waiting music.

Your AlpenBank app

During the call, the assistant can show your full transaction list in the AlpenBank app and let you pick a transaction to ask about.

  1. Ask for your recent transactions, then say “Can you show my transactions in the app?” (or say yes when the assistant offers it).
  2. Click “Open AlpenBank app” and enter the 6-character access code the assistant reads out (valid for 5 minutes; this is not your 2FA PIN).
  3. Keep talking: the call continues in this tab while the app is open.

Talk to our voice assistant

Available 24 hours, 7 days a week

  1. Start the call and allow your microphone when the browser asks.
  2. Ask your question. For account details you'll give your customer ID, your registered phone number (say it with the country code, e.g. “four nine three zero…”) and a six-digit code.
  3. Say the code digit by digit when asked. The assistant never reads it back.

The assistant greets you in English. To switch to Vietnamese, say “Vietnamese”. To switch back, say “tiếng Anh” (or “English”). A full sentence in Vietnamese also works; short answers such as IDs and codes never change the language.
